    How to play

    Choose a waiting group, then a highlighted table or a table button. Drag any seated guest to a highlighted table to move their whole party. Time is held while dragging; release outside a table or press Escape to cancel. Moving costs 4 mood per guest, keeps their drinks, and leaves the old table dirty. You can also move parties using the menu under Other actions. Staff take orders, brew, and deliver automatically while working. Click guests to see their party’s drinks and relevant actions. The machine opens a list of named drinks. Pick one up to highlight its recipient, then click that guest or Deliver. Your tray holds up to three drinks. Load it at the counter, then click a highlighted table or its Serve button to deliver that table's drinks. Staff leave carried drinks alone. Cups, milk and pastries are finite: restock at the counter or pay extra for delivery. Specials change arrivals and visit length. Click a spill or warning marker to handle it yourself or delegate. Your physical tasks take time, while seating and decisions remain available. Unfinished supply orders are refunded at closing. Water refills one guest’s patience for $2; asking a social party for quiet reduces noise for 12 seconds. Bookings and requests open here too. Pause freezes timers while you decide.
